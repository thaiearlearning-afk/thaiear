About time

ThaiEar is a Thai-language listening resource built from complete sentences spoken at a natural pace and rhythm, to help you attune your ear to spoken Thai.

Gone are the days when language learning involved isolated words and vocabulary lists. ThaiEar lets you hear how Thai is actually spoken, sentence by sentence, learning vocabulary and the structure of the language in a realistic context.

ThaiEar’s listening resources are the perfect way to develop both listening comprehension and to practise your spoken Thai. You can listen to our topics both ways: Thai first then English, or English first then Thai. Thai-first is great for building understanding, but when it comes to actually being able to speak Thai, English-first is a must.

ThaiEar works best once you can already read Thai script. Reading comes first for a reason: the script is what tells you a syllable’s tone, so learning to read is how you understand where the tones actually come from. With that foundation in place, ThaiEar is built for the next stage — turning what you know into fluency.

Our approach follows what knowledge-acquisition research has pointed to for decades:

  • 👂Context — words stick far better as part of whole phrases and sentences than in isolation.
  • 👂Dual-coding — pairing what you hear with what you read engages more than one sensory modality at once, which strengthens learning reinforcement (Paivio).
  • 👂Active recall, also known as retrieval practice beats passive review for learning, which is why we enable our mp3 topics to be played not only Thai to English, but also English to Thai.

Every topic is designed around all three: real sentences, native-speed audio paired with the text and word glosses, and a recall step you can switch on once a topic feels familiar.

We hope you enjoy listening to ThaiEar.
