About time

ThaiEar is a Thai-language listening resource built from whole sentences spoken at a natural pace and rhythm, to help you attune your ear to spoken Thai.

Gone are the days when language learning involved isolated words and vocabulary lists. ThaiEar lets you hear how Thai is actually spoken, sentence by sentence, learning vocabulary and the structure of the language in a realistic context.

Your Thai learning experience is delivered by the ThaiEar Dynamic mp3 player which delivers audio to your exact specification (see Guide) and can be taken offline in our App.

Whether you make your own Custom playlists or listen by topic, ThaiEar’s listening resources are the perfect way to develop both listening comprehension and to practise your spoken Thai.

You can listen to our topics both ways: Thai first then English (also known as “Shadowing” i.e. listen and repeat), or English first then Thai (also known as “active recall” or “retrieval practice”). Thai-first is great for building understanding, but when it comes to actually being able to speak Thai, English-first is a must (see Guide).

ThaiEar’s design follows what knowledge-acquisition research has pointed to for decades:

  • 👂Context — words stick far better as part of whole phrases and sentences than in isolation.
  • 👂Dual-coding — pairing what you hear with what you read engages more than one sensory modality at once, which strengthens learning reinforcement (Paivio).
  • 👂Active recall, also known as retrieval practice beats passive review for learning.

ThaiEar is designed around all three:

Whole sentences, native-speed audio paired with the text and word glosses, and the training of active recall. Topics and playlists can be played not only Thai to English, but also English to Thai, improving your ability to produce spoken Thai, and our reading course has multiple quizzes at each step.

We hope you enjoy listening to ThaiEar.
