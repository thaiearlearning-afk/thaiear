Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This policy explains how ThaiEar ("we", "us") — a trading name of Toby Ralph, a sole trader with a business address at 4th Floor, Silverstream House, 45 Fitzroy Street, Fitzrovia, London W1T 6EB — collects and uses your personal data when you use thaiear.com. We are the data controller, registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (reference ZC176414). For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at support@thaiear.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier.
  • Subscription & billing data — if you subscribe, our payment provider Stripe processes your payment. We store your Stripe customer/subscription identifiers and subscription status. We never see or store your card details.
  • Marketing preference — whether you have opted in to update emails.
  • Technical data — standard server/security logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) handled by our hosting/CDN provider.
  • Advertising measurement — if you arrive from one of our adverts and consent to advertising cookies, we record the identifier of that advert click and any campaign tags in the link you followed, together with the page you landed on and the site that referred you. If you then create an account we store that against your account, so we can tell which advert produced it. If you decline advertising cookies, none of this is stored and nothing of ours is kept on your device. See section 6.
  • Signup location (approximate) — when you create an account we record an approximate location worked out from your internet connection: country, city and network provider. This tells us which countries our visitors come from. It applies to every new account, whether or not you arrived from an advert. We do not store your IP address in this record, and it is deleted automatically if you delete your account.

2. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the service (sign-in, access to content) — performance of a contract.
  • To take payment and manage your subscription — performance of a contract.
  • To send update/offer emails — only with your consent (you can withdraw it any time).
  • To measure whether our advertising works — only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from section 6.
  • To understand where our users come from (the approximate signup location in section 1) — legitimate interests. We keep it deliberately coarse, never store the IP address it was derived from, and delete it with your account. You can object to this at any time by emailing us.
  • To keep the service secure and meet legal obligations — legitimate interests / legal obligation.

3. Who we share it with (processors)

We use trusted providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication and our database (EU-hosted).
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Google — "Sign in with Google" authentication.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, content delivery and security.
  • MailerSend — sending sign-in and account emails.
  • MailerLite — sending update emails (only if you opt in).
  • Google Ads — measuring whether our adverts work (only if you consent to advertising cookies; see section 6).

We do not sell your personal data. Some providers may process data outside the UK/EU — including Google and Stripe in the United States; where they do, appropriate safeguards (such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place.

4. How long we keep it

We keep account and subscription data for as long as you have an account, and as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes after that. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

The advertising-measurement and approximate-location records described in section 1 are held against your account and are deleted automatically when your account is deleted.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email support@thaiear.com. You can also delete your account from your account page.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use two kinds of storage on your device. You are asked about the second kind the first time you visit, and nothing in that category is set unless you agree to it.

Strictly necessary — always on

These make the site work. They are exempt from the consent requirement and cannot be turned off.

  • Sign-in session (sb-…-auth-token) — keeps you logged in.
  • Your settings and saved work — playlists, progress, playback preferences and any topics you have downloaded for offline listening. These stay on your device.
  • Your cookie choice (te_consent) — so we do not ask you again. Kept for 6 months.

Advertising — only with your consent

If you agree, we use these to measure whether an advert you clicked led to anything. They are not used to build a profile of you, and refusing them changes nothing about what you can read, use or listen to.

  • Google Ads measurement (_gcl_au, _gcl_aw) — set by Google so an advert click can be matched to what happened afterwards. Kept for up to 90 days.
  • Our own advert record (te_attrib) — the identifier of the advert click that brought you here, held on your device for up to 30 days. If you create an account we store it against that account so we know which advert produced it.

We apply Google Consent Mode, so if you refuse, Google's tags are told not to store anything or use your data for personalisation.

Changing your mind

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, with no effect on anything you have already done here.

What we do not do

We do not use advertising cookies to build advertising profiles, we do not sell or share your data with data brokers, and we do not use third-party analytics that track you across other websites.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
