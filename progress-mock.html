This page is designed as a tool for you to track your progress. We recommend each time you listen to a topic in full to add “one progress” to that topic — this is found above the mp3 player. After 3 to 5 complete listens of a topic, consider listening to it English-first. Practising producing the Thai yourself will build confidence and fluency.
