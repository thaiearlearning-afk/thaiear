Khûen & long (ขึ้น & ลง)

ขึ้น and ลง are the ordinary words for up and down, and Thai hangs them off other words to do two quite different jobs. Stuck on an adjective they mean more or less than before — ดีขึ้น is getting better, นอนน้อยลง is sleeping less than you used to. After a verb about thinking or feeling, ขึ้นมา says the thought arrived on its own: นึกขึ้นมาได้ is it suddenly coming back to you. Nothing moves upward in either case.

1 นอนsleeping…
ช่วงนี้เขานอนน้อยลง
chûang-níi khǎo nawn nói long
He's been sleeping less lately.
ช่วงนี้/ chûang-níi=latelyเขา/ khǎo=he/she/theyนอน/ nawn=sleepน้อยลง/ nói long=less
ขึ้น = going up, ลง = going down, and Thai attaches them to the adjective to mean 'more' or 'less' over time. น้อยลง is 'less than before', not simply 'little'.

3 นึกremembered…
นึกขึ้นมาได้ค่ะ
núek khûen maa dâi khâ
I suddenly remembered.
นึก/ núek=think / recallขึ้น/ khûen=upมา/ maa=comeได้/ dâi=canค่ะ/ khâ=polite(f)
ขึ้นมา after a verb about thinking or feeling says the thought turned up on its own — it just came to you. Nothing physically goes up.
