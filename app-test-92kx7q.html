How to install

  1. Tap Download above, then open the downloaded ThaiEar.apk (from the download notification or your Files app).
  2. Android will warn it’s from an unknown source — tap Settings and turn on “Allow from this source”, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect pops up, choose “Install anyway” (it only flags it because it’s not from the store yet).
  4. Open ThaiEar and you’re in.

This is a private link for testing — please don’t share it around. It’s a normal, safe app; Android just shows those warnings for anything not installed via the Play Store.
