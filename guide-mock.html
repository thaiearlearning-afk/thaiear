Thai first / English first: Two separate “sides” of the Dynamic player. Each with their own dynamic mp3 file, enabling you to have two audio streams set up — one for comprehension (Thai first) and one for practising recall and speaking (English first).

Thai repeats: Choose how many times a given Thai sentence is repeated.

Pauses: Adjust the pause length between sentences (shorter pauses give you less time to recall, but are good for passive review).

English sentence toggle (on Thai first side): Choose to remove the English entirely.

Include selected sentences: On a topic page, exclude any sentences not relevant to you, the Dynamic mp3 will construct your audio without it.

Sync: sync your Dynamic mp3 player settings across all Thai first or English first topics and playlists.

+1/−1: skip forward or back to next or previous sentence.

Spaced repetition

For most learners, ThaiEar could represent a year or even two years of quality study material. A key message of this guide is to discourage you from rushing, hopping from one topic to the next. If you chase two rabbits, you will catch neither.

Instead, language learning is best reinforced with spaced repetition, which means revisiting the topic again and again over time. To implement this approach, start by selecting a topic and revisit it several times over the course of a week or more. This focused approach is sure to build familiarity and confidence for your chosen topic. Once you are confident with a topic’s content, then you can introduce the next topic and make that your new primary focus.

Crucially, whilst studying any new topic, ensure you revisit topics you have already covered from time to time to consolidate and maintain your language base.

Revisiting previous topics can be as simple as just listening. Listening like this without a specific goal is known as passive review and has a good evidence base for consolidating learning. You can passively review ThaiEar’s sentences at home or on the go, making it highly flexible.

If you can be patient, adopting a consistent and measured approach to your learning, you will surely build an unshakeable foundation in the Thai language.
