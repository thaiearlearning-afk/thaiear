Listen offline on iPhone & iPad

On iPhone you don’t need to install anything to listen offline. Add ThaiEar to your Home Screen straight from Safari, and you can download whole topics and playlists to listen with no internet. It’s free and takes about a minute.

  1. Open thaiear.com in Safari.
    This only works in Safari — not Chrome or another browser.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up. It’s at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, or the top on an iPad.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as web app switched on — it usually is already.
    This is the important one: it’s what lets ThaiEar open full-screen and work offline. If you turn it off, it just opens as a Safari bookmark and offline downloads won’t be available.
  5. Tap Add in the top corner. The ThaiEar icon now sits on your Home Screen.
  6. Open ThaiEar from your Home Screen — it opens full-screen, ready to go.

Then turn on offline downloads

Do this once, from the ThaiEar you just added to your Home Screen:

  1. Open ThaiEar from your Home Screen, tap the menu (top-right) and choose ThaiEar App to come back to this page.
  2. Switch on Offline downloads in the box at the top of the page.
    That box only appears when you’ve opened ThaiEar from your Home Screen — so if you can’t see it, you’re still in Safari.
  3. Open any topic and tap Download for offline — or, on the home page, tap the circle on each topic you want and hit Download.
Downloaded topics play with no internet — even in airplane mode. On iPhone these are kept in the app’s own storage; if your phone runs very low on space, iOS may clear them, and you can simply reconnect and tap Download again.

Prefer a direct download?

You can also install ThaiEar for Android directly, without the Play Store — handy on phones without Google services. Your phone will show a security warning when you install it — that’s completely normal for any app installed this way. Here’s how:

Download ThaiEar for Android (.apk)

Version 1.4 · 8.6 MB · Android 7.0+

  1. Tap Download ThaiEar for Android (.apk) above.
  2. When it finishes, open the file. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser — tap Settings → Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If you see “Unsafe app blocked” or a Play Protect prompt, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open ThaiEar and sign in as normal.

You stay in control — you can switch that “allow” permission back off afterwards.

Everything works in your browser today — the app adds offline listening and lock-screen playback.
