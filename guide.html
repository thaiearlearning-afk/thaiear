How to make the most of ThaiEar

The ThaiEar syllabus is designed to take a relative beginner who is familiar with the Thai script to an intermediate level of speaking.

Not familiar with the script yet? Start with our Read Thai section. It teaches the alphabet, the vowels and the tone rules from zero, with audio for every letter and a test at every step. Learn to read first — the script is what tells you each word's tone.

Chewing it over

For most learners, ThaiEar could represent a year or even two years of quality study material. A key message of this guide is to discourage you from rushing, hopping from one topic to the next. If you chase two rabbits, you will catch neither.

Our material is divided into 10 to 40 sentences per topic or subtopic. A topic could therefore represent anywhere between 10 and 80 new words for you — a worthy challenge. So how to rise to this challenge?

Language learning is best reinforced with spaced repetition, which means revisiting the topic again and again over time. To implement this approach, start by selecting a topic and revisit it several times over the course of a week or more. This focused approach is sure to build familiarity and confidence for your chosen topic. Once you are confident with a topic’s content, then you can introduce the next topic and make that your new primary focus.

Crucially, whilst studying any new topic, ensure you revisit topics you have already covered from time to time to consolidate and maintain your language base.

Revisiting previous topics can be as simple as just listening. Listening like this without a specific goal is known as passive review and has a good evidence base for consolidating learning. You can passively review ThaiEar’s sentences in a number of circumstances, making it highly flexible.

If you can be patient, adopting a consistent and measured approach to your learning, you will surely build an unshakeable foundation in the Thai language.
